# AP automation buyers (demo)

Only at Fit A/B accounts that reach Tier A or B ([AP signal weights and tiers](ap-signal-weights.md)).

| Role | Titles | Why them | Tier A | Tier B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Economic buyer | CFO, VP Finance, Head of Finance | Signs the contract, owns the finance budget | Yes | If no controller |
| Champion | Controller, Director of Finance, Head of Accounting, Finance Systems Manager | Owns the close and the AP process, runs the evaluation | Yes | First choice |
| User | AP Manager, AP Lead, Accounts Payable Supervisor | Feels the manual invoice work every day | Yes | No |

## Persona fit
- **Fits:** an in-house role at the account in one of the 3 rows above, currently in the seat.
- **Bonus:** new in the role (120 days or less).
- **Does not fit:** AP clerks and specialists (a hiring signal, not a buyer), external accountants and auditors, IT without a finance-systems remit, people who left the company.

## Contact rules
- Verified work email: email plus LinkedIn steps.
- No verified email: LinkedIn steps only. Never a guessed email.
