# AP automation ICP (demo)

The company we sell to: a mid-market business that pays many supplier invoices, keeps finance in-house and runs an ERP our product connects to.

## Fit A: all of these
- **Region:** headquartered in the United States.
- **Size:** 100 to 1,000 employees.
- **Industry:** wholesale and distribution, manufacturing, construction, or transportation and logistics. These pay hundreds of supplier invoices a month.
- **ERP:** runs NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or Acumatica (seen in job postings or email DNS records).
- **Finance:** an in-house finance team (a CFO, controller or AP staff of its own).

## Fit B
Region, size, industry and in-house finance match, but the ERP is unknown or a different one.

## Fit C (no spend)
- Outside the US, under 100 or over 1,000 employees, or another industry.
- Excluded types: accounting, bookkeeping and audit firms; staffing and recruiting agencies; fractional-CFO firms; AP automation, payments and ERP vendors or their implementation partners; government and public sector.
- Unknown size or industry means "unknown", not Fit C: hold and enrich.
