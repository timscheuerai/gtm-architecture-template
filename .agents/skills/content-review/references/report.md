# Review output

Keep the user-facing answer short enough to act on. The saved report can hold evidence tables and method detail. Scale this shape to a single piece, a channel or a whole waterfall; skip fields that do not apply.

## Verdict and coverage

Lead with the main constraint and the strongest next decision. Name the account, platforms, goal, publication window and observation date and timezone.

| Data or content inspected | Source and date | Coverage or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native performance | Exact export, native read or raw snapshot | Fields, window, sample size, missing permissions |
| Published content | URLs and the segments actually inspected | Full video, selected segments, transcript only or unavailable |
| Product outcomes | Attribution path and cohort | Which conversions are measured, untracked or too recent |
| Production | Brief, handoff or reported time | Measured work, runtime, waiting or unknown |

## Pieces and findings

Keep stable piece IDs and links. Show only the metrics that explain the decision, with denominators and comparison window. Review losers and inconclusive pieces as well as winners. For each important finding state the observation and exact source, the comparison and confidence, the likely interpretation and a credible alternative, and the proposed change with the evidence that would distinguish it.

For several platforms, show each one first, then ask where the waterfall's promise, resource, timing or conversion handoff breaks.

## Next experiment and feedback

| Experiment | Hypothesis and changed variable | Metric and comparison | Review point and decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A concrete change to the next piece or workflow | What is tested and what stays comparable | Native goal metric or measured effort, with a real baseline | Date or evidence threshold; keep, change or inconclusive |

Name any earlier experiment now resolved and what stays open. Give the next brief a directly usable instruction.
