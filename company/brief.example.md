## Calibration set

Choose ten companies you know. Include clear fits, near misses, hard exclusions, incomplete evidence and a suppressed account. Record your expected company/persona decision **before** running a model. Differences are prompts to inspect evidence and rubric interpretation.

## Output of this first pass

A saved filter set, accepted/held account list with reasons, one buyer per accepted account where supported, one concrete draft, a frozen experiment and a list of unresolved setup/mapping steps.

Use the content context for public storytelling and voice. A private account result does not automatically become public proof.
